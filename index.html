Background & Approach

About

I am a Materials Engineering graduate from Monash University with a foundation in Mechanical and Advanced Manufacturing Engineering from SASTRA University. My core focus is failure analysis, root cause investigation, and closing the gap between material behaviour and process performance — in environments where getting it wrong has real consequences.

At SisterWorks Inc., I conducted structured RCA on safety-critical tram fleet components, isolated failure modes under mechanical load and fatigue, and translated findings directly into corrective action — through supplier liaison, compliance-mapped material selection (EN 45545-2, AS 1530), and production-ready documentation. At Falcon 3D, I commissioned a high-temperature FDM system from first principles, developed PEEK process parameters through structured DOE, and extended the asset's reliable operating envelope from 340°C to 450°C — delivering validated production capability at 81% below commercial cost.

I understand the language heavy industry speaks: equipment commissioning, process parameter optimisation, asset life extension, and structured documentation from observation through to corrective action. I work well in demanding, shift-based environments and take ownership of problems end-to-end.

Currently seeking graduate or entry-level roles in process and materials engineering across resources, heavy manufacturing, and infrastructure. Open to relocation and FIFO arrangements.
