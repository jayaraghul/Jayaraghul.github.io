# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two primary audiences, often overlapping:

1. **Industry recruiters and hiring managers** in engineering, manufacturing, and materials sectors. Scanning fast; need immediate proof of technical credibility and real-world impact. Ask: "Can this person do the job?" within 10 seconds.
2. **Academic supervisors and research peers** at universities and R&D institutions. Reading slower; care about rigour, methodology, and publication credibility.

Both audiences should leave feeling: "This person is technically serious, easy to work with, and clearly accomplished."

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Jayaraghul Thangaraj Kannan, Materials & Process Engineer and Monash University graduate. The site exists to convert qualified visitors into interview requests or research conversations. Success looks like a recruiter forwarding the link with "we should talk to this person."

## Brand Personality

Warm, human, accomplished. Technical depth without sterility. Approachable confidence, not arrogance. Feels like someone you'd genuinely enjoy working alongside.

Voice: first-person, direct, specific. No generic engineering clichés. Every sentence earns its place.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap/template portfolio clones (navy + identical card grid)
- Overly decorative or animation-heavy designs that distract from the engineering work
- Dark hacker/terminal aesthetic (wrong field, wrong signal)
- Corporate grey sterility that loses the human warmth

## Design Principles

1. **Work speaks first.** The engineering projects carry the credibility. Design surfaces them; design never competes with them.
2. **Earned warmth.** Personal touches (photo, hobbies, tone of copy) are deliberate signals that there's a person here, not just a CV.
3. **Quiet confidence.** Typography, whitespace, and restraint over ornamentation. Decoration is a sign of insecurity; substance is the brand.
4. **Both audiences in one pass.** Fast-scan hierarchy for recruiters (name, role, key achievements visible immediately) and depth for readers who click through (project case studies, publication, technical detail).
5. **Nothing for free.** Every element justifies its presence. If it doesn't inform or direct, it gets cut.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Strong contrast ratios, keyboard navigable, screen reader friendly alt text on all images. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion` for any animations.
